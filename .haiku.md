The haikus for Agent in the World. When a new world is built, agent.sh picks one of them and shows it to the player once.

How to add one: write the three lines, then a "trail:" line, then a blank line. The trail lists the rooms on the way to the destination, outermost first, and its last word is the destination room itself. Every trail word should appear in the haiku. If the haiku says "egress", end the trail with it. For example "trail: dream hopes egress" builds .../dream/hopes/egress.

Lost in a maze
vague memory of a dream
hopes of egress
trail: dream hopes egress

Walls without a door
a whisper through the silence
follow where it fades
trail: whisper silence

Night over the maze
a lantern burns in the mist
and the fog gives way
trail: mist lantern

Thread through the tangle
each step a half-lost story
pull it to the end
trail: thread story

Rain on ancient stone
old maps fade, the river knows
water finds its way
trail: river water

Doors that open in
sleep, a bridge of shifting sand
hope walks to egress
trail: bridge sand egress

Cold wind, narrow path
the compass spins, then settles
north was always home
trail: compass north

Moss on the old wall
the owl keeps watch till dawn
morning shows the gate
trail: owl dawn
